The Problem: Passive Consumption vs Active Retrieval

Most engineering students spend hours watching educational videos without testing their comprehension. Standard university exams happen weeks later, when it is too late to fix fundamental misconceptions. AI LearnMate bridges this gap through immediate Formative Assessment: turning lecture notes and transcripts into structured, adaptive diagnostic tests that pinpoint exactly where understanding breaks down.

How AI LearnMate Works

Key Educational Features
